Context: Jàng ("apprendre" in Wolof) is a student project (GET 409 Atelier IA, Swiss UMEF University, Dakar campus) by Cheikh BOYE and Adama DIOP: a WhatsApp corrector for Senegalese Bac physics-chemistry exercises, for Terminale S2 students in regional high schools who revise alone because they have no teacher. The student sends an exercise ID and their answer; Jàng explains the first mistake, checked against a reference answer, and sends a similar exercise — the same evening, in under 100 KB of data. Persona: Aminata Diallo, 17, Terminale S2 in Kaffrine, entry-level Android phone, daily data bundle.
