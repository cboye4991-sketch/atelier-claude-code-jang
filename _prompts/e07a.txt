Context: New project "Jàng web" (the fil rouge project of this workshop): the web MVP of Jàng, a corrector for Senegalese Bac physics-chemistry exercises (Terminale S2) for students in regional high schools who revise alone without a teacher, on entry-level Android phones with a small daily data bundle. Three pages: Accueil, Exercices (catalogue of exercises with IDs JNG-PC-xx that match the Dify knowledge base "Jang_KB_v1", filters by subject, and a WhatsApp-style "Corrige mon exercice" chat), Contact (volunteer teachers). The correction comes from an existing Dify workflow called through POST https://api.dify.ai/v1/workflows/run with inputs.query; the answer is in data.outputs.text, or data.outputs.message_erreur when the request is out of scope; Gemini free tier can take 4 to 40 s. Phase 2 will add a real WhatsApp channel. Stack: Vite + React + TypeScript, Firebase Hosting + Cloud Functions (2nd gen) holding the Dify key as a secret, later Firestore for anonymous progress. Visual identity: the /jang-brand skill ("le cahier corrigé").
